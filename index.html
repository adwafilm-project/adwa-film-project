THE CHRONICLES

Road to Sovereignty

1889

The Treaty of Wuchale

A dispute over Article XVII—where the Italian version claimed Ethiopia was a protectorate, while the Amharic version maintained independence—sparks the conflict.

1895

The Mobilization

Emperor Menelik II issues a national call to arms. From every corner of the empire, over 100,000 soldiers begin the march toward the northern front.

JAN 1896

Siege of Mekelle

Empress Taytu Betul leads a strategic maneuver to cut off the Italian water supply, forcing a surrender and proving Ethiopian tactical superiority.

MAR 1, 1896

The Battle of Adwa

In a single day of intense combat, the Ethiopian forces decisively defeat the Italian army, securing the nation's permanent independence.

Cinematic Sovereignty

We are using state-of-the-art cinematography to bring the 19th-century Ethiopian court and the rugged highlands to life with absolute authenticity.
